Research

MSc Thesis

Artificial Intelligence techniques to support monitoring of infrastructures for Network Function Virtualization (NFV)

This thesis project has been realized by Dawit Mezemir Anelay in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the MSc in Computing Science at University of Pisa, under the joint supervision of Tommaso Cucinotta (Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna) and Davide Bacciu (University of Pisa).

The work supports the transition from physical appliances to cloud-native virtual machines and containers through AI methods for performance monitoring, troubleshooting, and capacity planning in telecom NFV infrastructure.
